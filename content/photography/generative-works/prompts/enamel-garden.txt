Use case: stylized editorial illustration for a personal photography and visual-art website.

Create one original, finished portrait artwork in a 2:3 vertical composition. The central subject is an elegant Persian nightingale perched among abundant flowers. Reimagine the bird and foliage through the visual language of traditional Iranian minakari (Persian enamelwork): deep cobalt and lapis-blue enamel fields, turquoise details, fine ivory linework, restrained coral-pink and warm gold accents, intricate Persian floral and arabesque motifs. The flowers should feel lush, varied, organic, and gently asymmetric rather than arranged in a rigid geometric pattern. The bird should remain expressive and recognizable, with refined decorative feather patterns inspired by Iranian craftsmanship.

Mood: poetic, intimate, luminous, culturally grounded, slightly dreamlike.
Composition: full-frame artwork, strong but graceful focal point, naturally irregular floral framing, enough visual breathing room, no border.
Surface: rich glazed enamel impression with subtle handcrafted texture and fine detailing, not photorealistic, not 3D.
Palette: cobalt, lapis, turquoise, ivory, muted coral, dusty rose, and small warm-gold accents.
Avoid: text, Persian calligraphy, letters, logos, signatures, watermarks, mockup frames, checkerboard backgrounds, overly symmetrical mandala layouts, generic Western botanical illustration, copied contemporary artist style.

The result must look polished enough to be displayed as the first artwork in a curated personal collection called “Generative Works”.
