I’m Amir Hossein Hasanzadeh, a software engineer focused on backend development. Most of my work revolves around building web services and products—from shaping application logic and system architecture to solving the challenges that emerge in enterprise-scale projects.

Python is at the center of my toolkit, and I’ve worked across the backend stack with technologies such as Django, FastAPI, Redis, and Kafka. I’ve also built Telegram bots and worked on many of the moving parts behind web products. To me, software engineering is more than assembling technologies; it is the craft of turning complex problems into systems that are clear, reliable, and maintainable.

My world extends beyond code. Writing, photography, learning, and preserving things worth remembering are also important parts of my life. This website is where those threads come together—a home for my projects, experiences, notes, photographs, and anything else that reflects how I see and engage with the world.

I think of this space as a personal reference that will keep evolving over time: not a polished, definitive showcase, but an honest record of what I build, what I learn, and what matters to me.
